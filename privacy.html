Privacy Policy

Mini Games Hub is committed to providing a fun, transparent, and private browser gaming space. This document details how we interact with your browser, cookies, and local data files.

⚡ Zero Registration & Zero Tracking: We do not collect names, email addresses, cookies, IP records, or player identities. The platform is entirely static and client-side.

1. What Data is Collected?

We do not operate a centralized backend database. No personal credentials, session identifiers, or tracking scripts are generated or kept by our host. You can play all games anonymously without creating an account.

2. Client-Side Browser Storage (localStorage)

To deliver a premium, personalized gaming environment, the website stores small snippets of text data locally within your web browser using HTML5 localStorage. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to us:

  • Gameplay History: Tracks which games you played and when, rendering the "Last: X minutes ago" tags on game cards.
  • High Scores & PBs: Stores your speed values, click counts, or test completions (like in Click Counter or Word Game) to show your Personal Best records.
  • Simulation Budgets: Stores designed member thicknesses, foundation data, or draft saves in games like Structural Engineer.

3. Cookies & Analytics

This site does not drop marketing cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel). Some browser preferences or layout configurations may utilize local device cache files to optimize render times.

4. External Services

Our pages load static assets, fonts, and script resources from reliable external networks (e.g., Google Fonts, static CDN assets). When your browser fetches these components, the external service may register request diagnostics like your IP address, subject to their respective privacy terms.

5. Clearing Your Data

Because your games history and score records are kept inside your local web browser, you can clear them at any time by:

  • Clearing your web browser's cache and site data under your privacy settings.
  • Pressing F12 to open developer tools, selecting the 'Application' or 'Storage' tab, choosing 'Local Storage', and clicking 'Clear All'.

6. Updates to this Policy

We may make minor corrections to this statement to reflect changes in added mini-games or new storage variables. Any updates will be reflected on this page with the revision timestamp below.
